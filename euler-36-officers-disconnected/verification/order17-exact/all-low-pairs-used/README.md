# No size-17 maximal arrangement uses all low-low pairs

This audit excludes order-six maximal arrangements of seventeen
placements with minimum label frequency two in which every pair of
degree-two labels from distinct groups occurs in a placement.
All 17 native Kissat proofs passed independent DRAT-trim replay.
This is not a Lean replay.

## Reduction

A low is a label of degree two; every group contains one because its
degrees add up to 17. Suppose there are two lows x,y in some group.
Every low in each other group is used with both x and y. Encode it as
two bits indicating which of the two words through x and which of the
two through y contains it. Two lows from different groups share a
word only if the bits agree in one place, and they cannot agree in
both places by pair injectivity. Three lows chosen in three other
groups would give a triangle of Hamming-distance-one bit pairs,
impossible. Thus each group has exactly one low. Each of the five
other labels has degree exactly three since the degree sum is 17.

The four lows lie in one word: if some word contains exactly three
lows, the fourth must use three separate words to pair with them
without repeating a pair among those three. If no word has at least
three lows, six words with two lows are needed, exceeding the eight
low incidences available. Normalize the all-low word to 0000. Each
low has one additional word, distinct from all the other ones.

Order these four one-low words by the position of their zero. In each
coordinate, their other three entries have one of five equality
patterns up to relabeling: 111, 112, 121, 122, 123. Enumerate the
5⁴ possible combinations, reject repetitions of projected pairs,
then identify simultaneous permutations of coordinates and of the
corresponding zero-indexed words, renaming free symbols in every
coordinate by order of first occurrence. The resulting 17 diagrams
are generated by `sources/allused_diagrams.py`. The first word of
each is 0111, the second pinned word in the base encoding. Suffix
degree sorting does not constrain fixed labels; ties among the
unpinned incidence rows and columns can be resolved without altering
the diagrams, as in the size-16 audit.

For every diagram, the exact CNF encodes pair injectivity, maximality,
the 17-placement cap, minima of two, exactly five units of excess
degree per group, and the pinned degree/profile conditions and words.
The added rectangle implications are consequences of maximality:
an unused pair x,y leaves at least 6−d(x)−d(y) candidate labels
in each remaining group, increased by any common neighbor. With a
candidate label z on one side, all pairs with the other side must be
used, giving the degree lower bounds in the propagation clauses.

## Reproduce

```sh
python -m pip install python-sat==1.8.dev24
python sources/allused_diagrams.py
python verify.py --solve /path/to/kissat --checker /path/to/drat-trim
```

The proof was run with
[Kissat 4.0.4](https://github.com/arminbiere/kissat/tree/rel-4.0.4)
and [DRAT-trim 2e3b2dc](https://github.com/marijnheule/drat-trim/tree/2e3b2dc0ecf938addbd779d42877b6ed69d9a985).
The case manifest records exact DIMACS, proof and replay-log SHA-256
digests and proof sizes. The binary proofs are not bundled.
