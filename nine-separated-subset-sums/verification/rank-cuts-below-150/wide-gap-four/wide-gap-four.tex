\section{\texorpdfstring{The normalized wide branch requires
\(x_8-x_7\ge4\)}{The normalized wide branch requires x\_8-x\_7\textbackslash ge4}}\label{the-normalized-wide-branch-requires-x_8-x_7ge4}

This appendix strengthens the previous wide terminal-pair restriction.
Let \(Y=x_7,\ X=x_8,\ M=x_9\), with the established final slice
\(148\le X<149,\ 149\le M<150\). Normalize the terminal short pair by
moving its two coordinates inward around its fixed midpoint. As detailed
in previous-e3-and-mixed-signs.md, this preserves all labeled subset
comparisons and the wide condition. Hence we may assume \(M=X+1\). Write
\(e=X-Y\). The previous proof rules out the wide band \(2\le e<3\) by
its forbidden half-sum interval and a signed-lift comparison. Here we
also rule out \(3\le e<4\).

\subsection{Exact displacement in the dense signed-rank
interval}\label{exact-displacement-in-the-dense-signed-rank-interval}

Let the positive full nine-sign magnitudes be sorted as \(D_j\), one
magnitude per pair of complementary words. Ranks and strict separation
uniquely determine their positive oriented words. The established
central envelope is \[
  D_j=2j+\theta_j,\qquad -1\le\theta_j\le\delta<1
  \quad(1\le j\le64),
\] with \(\delta=M-149\). Here \(D_{j+1}-D_j\ge2\) globally by two
complementary rank steps, so \(\theta_j\) is nondecreasing. Consequently
for ranks \(i<i'\le64\), \[
 2(i'-i)\le D_{i'}-D_i<2(i'-i)+2 .
\] In particular any observed \textbf{positive} full signed lift
\(D_i+2e\) for \(e\in[m,m+1)\), \(1\le i\le i+m\le64\), is exactly
\(D_{i+m}\). The rank displacement is at most \(m\) by global minimum 2
spacing, so its target is inside the valid envelope. A smaller
displacement would give a difference strictly below \(2m\). For a
positive lift \(D_i-2e\) with \(i\le64\) and \(i-m\ge1\), the same proof
gives exactly \(D_{i-m}\).

For \(q=50,53,60,63\) the forced formulas have top signs \[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
\text{rank}&q-1&q&q+1\\
\text{top signs}&(v_7,+,-)&(v_7,-,+)&(-w_7,+,+).
\end{array}
\] Here the first two values \(v_q-1,v_q+1\) differ exactly by 2 and
have the same first-six signed prefix and seventh sign. With \(v_7=+\),
switch the top pattern \(\texttt{+-+}\) at \(q\) to \(\texttt{-++}\) at
fixed first-six signs; its positive lift is \(D_q+2e\). With \(v_7=-\),
switch \(\texttt{-+-}\) at \(q-1\) to \(\texttt{+--}\), giving
\(D_{q-1}-2e=D_q-2-2e\). The starting rank \(q-1\), rather than \(q\),
is essential.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{The band \(3\le e<4\) is
impossible}{The band 3\textbackslash le e\textless4 is impossible}}\label{the-band-3le-e4-is-impossible}

Put \(m=3\). If \(v_{53}(7)=-\), the lift \(D_{52}-2e>0\) has rank 49 by
the displacement rule and top signs \(\texttt{+--}\). But rank 49 is the
companion of rank 50 with last two signs \(+-\), a contradiction.
Therefore \(v_{53}(7)=+\) and its far lift \(D_{53}+2e\) is at rank 56
with top \(\texttt{-++}\).

If \(v_{60}(7)=-\), its first lift \(D_{59}-2e>0\) is also at rank 56,
but with top \(\texttt{+--}\), inconsistent with the positive word
already there. If \(v_{60}(7)=+\), its far lift \(D_{60}+2e\) is at rank
63 with top \(\texttt{-++}\), whose last two signs are \(++\),
inconsistent with the prescribed rank 63 having last signs \(-+\)
regardless of \(v_{63}(7)\). All these positive lifts lie in ranks 1
through 64 by the given bracketing argument. Positivity of the downward
lifts also follows numerically from \(D_{52}\ge103\), \(D_{59}\ge117\)
and \(2e<8\). This exhausts the signs and proves \(e\ge4\) in the
normalized wide branch.

\subsubsection{Independent cross-check using the prior mixed-sign
identities}\label{independent-cross-check-using-the-prior-mixed-sign-identities}

In this band those identities give \(v_{53}(7)=+\),
\(v_{60}(7)=-,\ w_{61}(7)=+\), and each physical gap
\(1\le g_k\le e-2\). The far positive two-plus lift \(D_{53}+2e\) is at
rank 55 or 56 even without the full envelope: its distance is in
\([6,8)\), steps are at least 2 and consecutive central steps are
strictly below 4. It cannot be 55, since its distance above \(D_{54}\)
is \(2(e-g_{161})\ge4\). So it must be \(D_{56}\), with top
\(\texttt{-++}\).

For \(w_{61}(7)=+\), its positive middle lift
\(D_{61}-2e=D_{60}+2g_{168}-2e\) lies strictly above \(D_{60}-6\) and at
most \(D_{60}-4\), using \(g_{168}\ge1,\ e<4,\ g_{168}\le e-2\).
Consequently it is exactly \(D_{58}\): it is below \(D_{59}=D_{60}-2\),
but any rank at or below 57 is at least 6 below \(D_{60}\). Its first
companion, another exact 2 lower, is \(D_{57}=D_{61}-2e-2\). The
positive first lift of the negative \(v_{60}\) is \(F=D_{60}-2e-2\),
with top \(\texttt{+--}\). It lies below \(D_{57}\) by \(2g_{168}\ge2\)
and strictly less than 10 below \(D_{60}\). Thus it is above \(D_{55}\)
and exactly \(D_{56}\). All three raw numbers are positive by
\(D_{60}\ge119\). The two top words conflict at \(D_{56}\), as above.

\subsection{Optional static sign-pattern count, not a chamber
search}\label{optional-static-sign-pattern-count-not-a-chamber-search}

For \(m\ge4\) one can impose the preceding deterministic lifted top
words, checking compatibility only on their ranks. The positive lift of
a plus \(v_q\) uses the rank \(q+m\) when it is in the envelope. A minus
\(v_q\) gives \(\texttt{+--}\) at \(q-1-m\) when \(m\le q-3\), which
ensures its positivity. A plus \(w\) gives \(\texttt{+-+}\) at \(q+1-m\)
and \(\texttt{++-}\) at \(q-m\) when \(m\le q-1\): the middle lift is
then positive by the lower rank envelope, and its positive predecessor
is one step lower (its rank is at least 2). No extraneous rule is
imposed when the guaranteed in-window or positivity conditions are
absent.

Optionally, two negative \(w\) words yield two positive all-plus full
words at ranks \(r,r'\). Writing \(T=Y+X+M\), their values have form
\(T-B,T-B'\) with positive first-six full forms \(B=Y+w,\ B'=Y+w'\). The
first-six signed-support half-difference is \(|D_r-D_{r'}|/2\). If the
rank difference is \(m\) or \(m+1\), the displacement identity places it
strictly within the forbidden interval \((e-1,e+2)\) from the two top
centers \(e,e+1\). This extra rule is a necessary sign filter.

The supplied small integer program checks just these top labels among
\(2^8\) sign choices. Nonzero pattern counts are neither feasible
chamber counts nor evidence of actual configurations. No interval beyond
\(e<4\) is excluded by this table.
