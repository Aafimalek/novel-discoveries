\noindent\textbf{Result.}
Let \(G\) be the unit-distance graph on the fixed set of 449 points
\href{https://github.com/bprzybocki/G449/tree/125bfda97ed1d336f84694de04dedcfb349c5164}{G449}
of Heule and Przybocki. Add six distinct new points in the plane,
and include every new unit edge. If the six points alone induce a
connected graph, the extension is five-colorable. The only
unresolved six-point extensions of this particular \(G\) have a
disconnected new-point graph, all new points on the grid below,
and at least two original neighbors at every new point.
This is a finite computation, not a Lean formalization.
It does not change the chromatic-number bounds for the plane.

\paragraph{Reduction.}
Write \(\zeta=e^{2\pi i/42}\). The
\href{https://github.com/Aafimalek/novel-discoveries/tree/234e0ce194fdfa544d4b551ccea9898b2239dc69/hadwiger-nelson/verification/g449/prime-seven-catalogue/five-point-extensions}{five-point extension theorem}
implies that every added point of a putative non-five-colorable
six-extension must have total degree at least five: otherwise
delete it, color, and restore it using a color absent from its
at most four neighbors. The earlier
\href{https://github.com/Aafimalek/novel-discoveries/tree/6376de4361b98f605326dd6275fa5f9e8b5dd3fb/hadwiger-nelson/verification/g449/prime-seven-catalogue/six-off-grid-extensions}{off-grid result}
and the
\href{https://github.com/Aafimalek/novel-discoveries/tree/f7be4c235d3d6bc073058db7fb201663ed7955e7/hadwiger-nelson/verification/g449/prime-seven-catalogue/six-grid-one-nonuniversal}{low-original-degree results}
reduce the search to six points of \(\tfrac17\mathbb Z[\zeta]\)
with at least two neighbors in \(G\) apiece. The exact grid direction
catalogue has 546 signed unit steps, so each candidate is obtained
by adding one of these steps to some point of \(G\).
Adding all steps and counting original neighbors gives exactly
7,056 outside candidates with at least two original neighbors.
Testing exact direction membership among them constructs
their full induced graph of 33,908 edges.

\paragraph{Complete connected enumeration.}
Order candidate IDs lexicographically by the integer coefficient
vectors and assign each induced connected candidate set to the
least ID's residue modulo 64. Its canonical parent deletes the
largest nonroot ID whose deletion leaves a connected induced
set. Such an ID exists by taking a nonroot leaf in a spanning
tree. Every augmentation is tried from the first current vertex
adjacent to the new one, and accepted as an enumeration step
only if it inverts the canonical parent. By induction from the
singletons this visits each unpruned connected set exactly once.
If \(S\) is an intermediate set and \(v\in S\), the completed
degree of \(v\) is at most \(b(v)+d_S(v)+6-|S|\), where
\(b(v)\) counts original neighbors and \(d_S(v)\) neighbors
inside \(S\). Below five, prune. This does not remove any prefix
of an eligible six-point set. At size six this is exactly the
necessary degree condition.

\paragraph{Positive certificates.}
Each of the 40 palettes is directly checked on every original edge.
For a fixed palette, a new vertex has the list of colors not used on
its original neighbors. A deterministic list-color test branches
on every still-allowed color of a vertex whose list has minimum
size, deleting that color from its remaining new neighbors.
A successful leaf is a valid coloring of the whole extension.
The 64 completed shards visited \textbf{11,652,641} eligible
connected sets of size six, and each was colored by at least one
palette. There were \(1{,}501{,}845{,}702\) frontier probes.
The adjudication is by accepted colorings; a miss by one or
several palettes is not a proof of non-five-colorability.

In the initial 38-palette run, shard 11 found three misses. A single
model colored the union of their eight new vertices; exact arithmetic
recomputed all 104,196 pair distances on the resulting 457 vertices
and checked its 2,257 induced edges. Its restriction to the original
vertices gives palette 39. The next bank miss was one six-point
set in shard 36. Its positive model was checked on all 2,242 induced
edges after recomputing all 103,285 pair distances; its restriction
gives palette 40. Both checked witnesses and the deterministic
coverage replayers are included. No solver is required for the
replay. A disconnected configuration must use a common base
palette across its components, which is not established by the
connected enumeration.
